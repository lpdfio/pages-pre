This page describes the support that comes with each Lpdf license tier. It forms part of the Lpdf License where that license refers to support terms.

How to get support

Tier Channel

Professional Email support@lpdf.io from the address on your portal account
Enterprise Email support@lpdf.io; escalation contacts are agreed at purchase

Licensing, billing and account questions go to license@lpdf.io at any tier, including Community.

Security issues. Email support@lpdf.io with SECURITY at the start of the subject line, or open a private vulnerability report on GitHub, at any tier including Community. Please do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability. We aim to acknowledge security reports within one business day.

Response times

Response times are targets for a first substantive response, not resolution times. A response means a human has read your report, reproduced it or asked for what is needed to reproduce it, and told you what happens next.

Business hours are 09:00–17:00 US Central Time, Monday to Friday, excluding United States public holidays. All targets below are measured in business hours or business days on that clock.

Severity Meaning Community Professional Enterprise
S1 — Blocked Document generation fails in a production system and no workaround is available Best effort 1 business day 4 business hours
S2 — Impaired Incorrect output or degraded behaviour, with a workaround available Best effort 2 business days 1 business day
S3 — Question Usage, integration, schema or documentation questions Best effort 3 business days 1 business day

We set the severity on receipt, and will tell you if we assess it differently from you. If you disagree, say so and we will discuss it — severity is about impact on your production system, not about urgency of feeling.

Community support carries no commitment. It is provided by us and by other users on a best-effort basis, and nothing on this page creates an obligation at that tier. In practice most questions get answered; that is a description of how we work, not a promise.

What support covers

In scope:

  • Defects in Lpdf — output that does not match the documented behaviour of the schema or SDK
  • Questions about documented behaviour, the XML schema, data binding, design tokens and SDK integration
  • Guidance on structuring a document to achieve a particular layout
  • License key, portal, renewal and provisioning problems
  • Regressions introduced by an Lpdf release

Out of scope:

  • Debugging your application, your data, or code that does not call Lpdf
  • Writing your documents or templates for you
  • Modified copies of Lpdf, or builds we did not publish
  • Problems caused by third-party libraries, runtimes or infrastructure
  • Unlicensed use — evaluation without a license key is unsupported, though we will always answer a question about how to obtain one

Work that falls outside this scope — template development, integration work, custom features — is available separately. Ask at license@lpdf.io.

What we need from you

The single largest factor in how quickly something is resolved is whether we can reproduce it. A report that includes the following is usually resolved in one exchange rather than four:

  • Lpdf version, SDK and language runtime version, operating system
  • The smallest document or code sample that reproduces the problem
  • What you expected, and what you got — attach the PDF if the output is wrong
  • Any error message in full, including the stack trace

Please do not send us production data. A reduced sample with representative structure is more useful and avoids putting your data anywhere it does not belong.

What this page does not promise

There is no availability or uptime commitment, at any tier. Lpdf runs entirely inside your own infrastructure — it makes no network calls and depends on no service we operate. There is nothing for us to keep running, so an uptime SLA would be meaningless. The portal at my.codesense.dev is used to issue and renew license keys, not to generate documents, so a portal outage does not stop your documents rendering.

We do not commit to fixing every reported defect, or to a fix within a stated time. We commit to responding within the times above, to telling you honestly whether and when we expect to fix something, and to giving you a workaround where one exists.

Response times apply while your license is current. Support ends when a subscription lapses, in line with the Lpdf License.

Enterprise

Enterprise licensees receive the response times in the table above by default. Where a Master Service Agreement is in place, its support terms replace this page for that licensee — including any agreed escalation path, named contact, or custom response targets.

To discuss an Enterprise license or custom support terms, contact enterprise@lpdf.io.

Changes to this page

We may update this page from time to time. Material reductions to the response times above will be notified by email to affected account holders at least 30 days in advance, and will not apply to a license term already paid for.
